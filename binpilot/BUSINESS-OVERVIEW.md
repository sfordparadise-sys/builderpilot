# BinPilot — Mimico Bin Cleaning: Content & Business Overview

A plain-language reference for the business, the model, and every piece of
information the website communicates. Content only — no design notes.

---

## 1. The business
**BinPilot** (operating as **Mimico Bin Cleaning**) is a local, family-run
curbside garbage-bin cleaning service run by **Steve**, who lives in Mimico and
has been in the neighbourhood since 2021. It serves **Mimico and New Toronto**
(west Toronto / Etobicoke).

Core promise: your garbage, recycling, and green bins get professionally cleaned
and sanitized **right at your curb**, with **zero effort from you** — you never
lift, haul, or touch anything.

## 2. The idea / the "why"
The pitch rests on one uncomfortable truth: **your bins are filthier than your
toilet.** They sit a few feet from your home, your kids, and your dog, and they
collect:
- **Bacteria** — E. coli and salmonella thrive in warm bin gunk
- **Flies, maggots, and wasps** — a forgotten food wrapper becomes a maggot
  nursery within 48 hours
- **Raccoons and skunks** — a smelly bin is a "dinner bell" that attracts wildlife
- **Smell, mould, and grime** that follow you around

Most people clean their toilet religiously but never touch the bin that's
arguably grosser. BinPilot fixes that.

## 3. The service / what they actually do
A **200°F high-pressure hot-water wash, inside and out** — not a garden-hose
rinse. Kills bacteria, mould, and odour on contact. All wastewater is captured
and disposed of properly. Done at the curb. Year-round, including winter (the
hot-water system powers through frozen gunk).

Bins covered (all three City of Toronto bins):
- **Garbage** (black)
- **Recycling** (blue)
- **Green Bin / organics**

## 4. The schedule / how it fits your week
- Garbage day is **Tuesday** (pickup empties your bins)
- BinPilot cleans **Wednesday** — right after pickup, while bins are empty
- You leave the empty bins out after Tuesday collection; you don't need to be home

## 5. Pricing
Flat, simple, no tiers or upsells. Every clean includes everything (full inside +
outside wash, sanitize, deodorize, lid + wheels, before/after photo, bins
returned to place):
- **1 bin — $25**
- **2 bins — $40**
- **3 bins — $50**

Two ways to buy:
- **Monthly plan** — recurring clean each month, priority scheduling
- **One-time** — single clean

No contracts. Cancel the monthly plan **anytime**, no fees.

## 6. The customer process (booking → done)
1. **Book in ~30 seconds** — pick your bins, choose monthly or one-time, drop your
   address. The site pre-fills your next clean date.
2. **Steve texts you back** — a real local neighbour confirms your spot (not a
   call centre or chatbot).
3. **Wednesday: the clean happens** — bins emptied Tuesday, washed Wednesday at
   your curb with the 200°F system.
4. **Before & after photo** — sent to your phone as proof; bins put back in place.

Cleaning method, step by step: **pre-treat → scrub → rinse → 200°F pressure
wash → spotless.**

## 7. Who's behind it (trust / local story)

right past Hillside. He and his family fell in love with the neighbourhood
instantly — the white-picket-fence feel, kids running around, neighbours having
drinks on front lawns, weekend garage sales, and the annual grilled-cheese
challenge. He wants his kids to grow up in a proper Canadian neighbourhood, and
cleaning his neighbours' bins is his way of looking after the place he loves.

Positioning: **a real local neighbour and family business — not a franchise or
call centre.**

## 11. The overall plan / business model
A neighbourhood-focused, subscription-friendly micro-service: hyper-local
(Mimico + New Toronto), one fixed weekly service day (Wednesday) for route
efficiency, simple flat pricing, low-friction booking (30-second form or a text),
and recurring monthly revenue as the goal — with one-time cleans as the on-ramp.
Trust and conversion are driven by local identity (Steve, a real neighbour),
proof (before/after photos, reviews), and urgency (limited weekly spots). The
model scales by adding route density on more streets in the same area rather than
spreading thin.
