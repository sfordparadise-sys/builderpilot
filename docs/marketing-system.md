## Step 2 — Your Audience (Who You're Talking To)

Every post is written for this person:

- Small-to-mid contractor or general contractor
- Running 1–5 active job sites at a time
- Coordinating crew via WhatsApp or text
- Losing time to missed updates, rework, and manual check-ins
- Has tried apps before — they didn't stick because they were too complex
- Just wants something simple that works on a real job site

**Their biggest frustrations:**
- "I'm always the last to know when something goes wrong"
- "My crew won't use the app"
- "I spend Sunday nights mentally reviewing every open site"
- "Change orders fall through the cracks and I lose money"
